How to Use These Infographics Effectively

01

Daily Bite-Sized Review

Pick 1–2 grammar or vocab infographics each day. Review them in the morning and again before bed.

02

Combine with Active Practice

After looking at an infographic, write 3–5 of your own example sentences using the pattern or words.

03

Print & Stick

Print the most useful charts and put them on your wall or desk for passive daily exposure.

04

Cross-Reference

Use Japanesetest4you for visuals + Hanabira/Japademy PDFs for structured lists and writing practice.
